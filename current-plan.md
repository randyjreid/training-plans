# 4-Week Rebuild Plan — Jul 27 to Aug 23, 2026

**Starting point (from Strava):** 20-min walk/run sessions, 2 min run / 2 min walk, ~1.2 mi. Last activity June 24 — one month gap, so Week 1 restarts at that level rather than progressing past it.

**Rest day: Monday.** Weekends carry your longest sessions, so Monday absorbs that load.

**Primary mode for walk/runs: Peloton Outdoor Audio classes.** App → Classes → Outdoor Audio → filter **Class Type = Walk + Run** (housed under the Running section), plus Duration and Difficulty as listed per session. Go-to instructors: **Kirsten Ferguson** (walk+run specialist), Susie Chan, Marcel Dinkins, Matt Wilpers, Becs Gentry. Class intervals won't exactly match the target ladder — that's fine; total easy run minutes matter more than exact ratios. **Walk whenever you need to, regardless of instructor cues.** To follow the exact ladder instead, use "Just Work Out" → Outdoor Running (GPS, no instructor) with a phone timer.

**Bad weather (decide day-of):** 1) Home treadmill — Running → Walk + Run Tread classes, same duration/difficulty (works on any treadmill via the app); keeps run progression intact. 2) Bike swap only as last resort, max once/week for a run day. 3) Long sessions: treadmill Walk + Run, or split 30 min tread + 20 min easy ride.

**Effort key:**
- **Easy** = full-sentence conversational pace. If you can't talk, slow down or walk.
- **Moderate** = short sentences only. Used sparingly.
- **Strength** = last 2 reps of each set challenging but clean form.

**Why almost everything is easy:** the 80/20 rule, plus you're rebuilding. Until you can run 30 min continuously, easy volume *is* the stimulus — hard run sessions now add injury risk without speeding progress. The gym strength days are your "hard" work for this block.

## Where this is heading

The ladder puts you at ~24 min of running per session by Week 4. **You will not hit 30 min continuous in 4 weeks — that's deliberate.** Realistic target: continuous 30 min around Week 8–10. Week 5 should be a cutback week (~-20% volume) before the next build.

**Rules baked in:** all cardio easy (>80/20 satisfied), no hard back-to-back days, 2 strength days/wk, weekly volume growth ≤10% after Week 1, weekday sessions ≤45 min in AM (done by 6:15) or PM (after 6:45) windows.

## Once you have a fitness watch (get one)

1. **Set an easy ceiling** — estimate max HR and set an easy ceiling ~75–80% of it (field-test later). HR will catch the days the talk test flatters you.
2. **Verify 80/20 by time-in-zone**, not feel.
3. **Track morning resting HR** — sustained +5 bpm = take the fallback or an extra rest day.
4. **Re-evaluate the Week 3 Friday "moderate finish"** — with HR data I'd build a proper low-dose tempo in Weeks 5+.

## Fat loss note

Fat loss will be won mostly in the kitchen. A modest calorie deficit (~300–500 kcal/day) with protein around 0.7–1 g per lb of target body weight protects muscle during weight loss. After a long training layoff, a physician check-in is sensible before adding harder efforts.
